# Sparkle Auto Web Art Direction

## Scope and dependency

Use only for Sparkle Auto projects and only after activating [`hdi-web-visual-art-director`](../hdi-web-visual-art-director/SKILL.md). This overlay adds brand constraints; it is not an image tool or a competing production pipeline. It does not apply the marketing-page structure to the functional booking application or owner dashboard.

## Direction

`Premium Stylized 3D × Egyptian Editorial Thinking × Fresh Blue Neo-Brutalist Web UI × Real Sparkle Auto Product Proof`

Arabic/RTL is primary; English/LTR is complete and independently composed. Product truth, accessibility, and usability are hard gates. Use real Sparkle screenshots only, keep the Owner identity specification and accepted internal anchor consistent, preserve the Sparkle palette and natural subject colors, and maintain deliberate typography, navy borders, controlled offset shadows, section rhythm, responsive recomposition, accessibility, and performance.

Generated 3D tells selected stories. Editorial composition shapes the idea. Native web components provide structure and action. Authentic product screens prove the product. Do not generate the finished page, real UI, commercial facts, or repeated presenter poses.

## Production split

- **GENERATE:** selected Owner acting scenes, complex branded props, car-wash environments, and editorial metaphors that the page needs.
- **CODE:** text, navigation, CTA, pricing, forms, FAQ, layout, outlines, shadows, numbers, diagrams, product frames, and simple functional spark trails.
- **REAL/REUSE:** approved logo, verified product screenshots, approved assets, and current commercial terms.
- **OMIT:** decorative or repeated elements that do not improve meaning, story, or proof.

If no approved recurring-identity asset exists, use the general skill's identity specification and calibration workflow. Never require the user to supply a reference image.

## Definition of done

The general skill's hard gates pass; Sparkle styling has not leaked into unrelated projects; Arabic and English compositions are inspected; Owner identity and physical contacts are coherent; real screenshots remain authentic and legible; palette, typography, borders, shadows, spark function, rhythm, mobile recomposition, accessibility, and measured delivery satisfy maintained project truth and any stricter existing gates.
